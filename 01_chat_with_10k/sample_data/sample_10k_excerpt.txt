ITEM 1. BUSINESS
Northwind Robotics, Inc. designs and sells warehouse automation systems,
including autonomous mobile robots and the software platform that
coordinates them. Revenue is recognised from hardware sales at delivery
and from software subscriptions ratably over the contract term. The
company sells primarily to logistics and retail customers in North
America and Europe.

ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS
The company's business depends on a small number of large customers.
In the most recent fiscal year, the top five customers accounted for
approximately 41% of total revenue. The loss of any major customer
could materially reduce revenue. The company also depends on a single
contract manufacturer for its robot drive units; supply interruptions
have previously delayed shipments by up to six weeks. Rapid changes in
tariffs on imported electronic components could increase cost of goods
sold and compress gross margins.

ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS
Total revenue increased 18% year over year, driven by a 32% increase in
software subscription revenue, which now represents 36% of total
revenue. Gross margin declined from 48% to 45% due to higher component
costs and expedited freight. Operating expenses increased primarily due
to investment in research and development for the next generation robot
platform.

ITEM 8. FINANCIAL STATEMENTS — NOTES ON LIQUIDITY
As of the fiscal year end, the company held $84 million in cash and cash
equivalents and had no outstanding borrowings under its $50 million
revolving credit facility. Management believes existing cash and the
credit facility are sufficient to fund operations for at least the next
twelve months.
